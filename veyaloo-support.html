Veyaloo Support and Privacy

Veyaloo is operated by Muhammed Nedim İpek. For support or privacy requests, email nedimiiipek@gmail.com.

Pairing and photo delivery

Veyaloo uses the name you enter, an app-generated device identifier and pairing records to connect your device to the person you invite. The service stores the photos, captions and touches you send so it can deliver them to the paired device. If notifications are enabled, the service uses a device push token to request updates via Apple Push Notification service on iOS or Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android.

Storage, access and deletion

Pairing data and photos are processed on Cloudflare infrastructure. The service keeps up to 30 recent photos per pair. Signed photo links expire after one hour. Disconnecting clears shared photos and messages from the service. Use the account deletion option in Settings to also remove your device record. If you report abuse, the report and identifiers needed to investigate it are stored separately.

Optional features

Where ads are available, Google AdMob processes information for ad delivery and measurement. Advertising choices are available in the app where applicable. Where paid features are available, the app store processes payment and RevenueCat can process purchase and entitlement information to enable or restore access.

Contact and requests

For help pairing devices, adding the widget or deleting data, contact the address above and include your platform and app version. You can also use this address to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Do not include passwords, private photos or invitation codes in support emails.
