VibeWarrior Privacy and Support

Muhammed Nedim İpek operates VibeWarrior. Contact nedimiiipek@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Game and account information

The online game server processes your nickname, language, gameplay answers, results and activity to operate the game, calculate rankings, and protect against abuse. Public rankings can display your nickname. If you use Google sign-in, account information from Google is used to authenticate you and provide your profile. The local Secret Word party game uses player names entered on the device.

Ads and purchases

Google AdMob supplies rewarded ads and may process identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions and diagnostics. The reward request sends a game user identifier and a reward request identifier to Google so that the server can verify the reward. Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase information and a game account identifier so that purchases can be verified.
