Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice covers Doner Empire: Kebab Tycoon, mobile base edition 0.25, and its support and privacy website. The game does not require a player account. This edition has no advertising or real-money in-app purchases.

Who is responsible

The controller is Muhammed Nedim İpek, operating as Woven Comet Technologies. For support or privacy requests, contact nedimiiipek@gmail.com.

Game information on your device

The game saves progress on your device: tutorial and campaign progress, orders, inventory, staff, buildings and fictional game currency. It also saves language, sound and display preferences, including reduced motion and battery-saving settings. Local backup or recovery files can preserve progress after a damaged save. A random local profile code, a local reward/transaction journal and round counters help keep local state consistent. These are not a registration account, and the game's base-edition code does not upload these records to a publisher-operated server. Game characters, money and health values describe the simulation, not your real contacts, finances or health.

Unity, advertising and purchases

The game is made with the Unity engine. The game's advertising, purchasing and gameplay analytics services are disabled in this edition; you do not need to watch an ad or make a purchase to play. We do not use the game to build advertising profiles or sell player information. For Unity's own explanation of its services and game-user privacy, see Unity’s game-player privacy notice. This notice does not replace the independent privacy information provided by your device operating system or app store.

Email support

When you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, and any name, screenshots, device details or attachments you provide. We use this information to answer the request, investigate the reported problem and maintain necessary correspondence. Sending a support email is optional; provide only what is needed and do not send passwords, card details or unrelated sensitive information. Without a return address or enough information about the problem, we may be unable to answer or resolve it.

Service providers and the website

Support correspondence is delivered and stored using Google Gmail and your chosen email provider. Our public support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security and may handle other request information under its privacy statement. Our own static pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, remote fonts or cookies. See Google’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement for their processing, retention and international-transfer information. Opening an external link takes you to that provider's service.

Purposes and legal grounds

Where the GDPR applies, processing necessary to provide the game or handle a related service request is based on performance of a contract or steps you request before one (Article 6(1)(b)). Other support correspondence and protection of our service rely on legitimate interests in answering enquiries, resolving faults and preventing misuse (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to your rights. Information required by law is processed under Article 6(1)(c). We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you solely by automated processing.

Storage, transfers and deletion

Local saves and preferences stay on the device until overwritten or removed. Clearing the app's storage on Android or deleting the app and its data on iOS removes local progress and settings; merely offloading an iOS app may retain its data. Device or app-store backup and restore features can retain or restore copies according to your settings. We cannot remotely erase device-only files. Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve and document the request, unless a legal duty, security incident or legal claim requires longer retention. We then delete information no longer needed. Google and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries, including outside the EEA; their linked notices explain the safeguards they use, including applicable adequacy frameworks and standard contractual clauses. We do not upload gameplay saves to these providers as part of support unless you choose to send them.

Your choices and rights

You can change game preferences and remove local data using your device controls. Contact nedimiiipek@gmail.com about personal information you have sent us. Depending on applicable law and the processing involved, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. We may request only the information reasonably needed to verify a request. You can complain to a competent data-protection authority; EU/EEA contacts are listed by the European Data Protection Board. Parents or guardians can contact us about personal information a child may have sent to support.

Changes

We will update this notice if the edition's features or data practices change. Any future advertising, online account or purchasing feature requires its own applicable disclosures and choices before use. The date above identifies this version of the notice.
