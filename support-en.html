Saves and progress

Where is my progress kept?

The game saves progress on your device and keeps a local backup for save recovery. Your device's backup and restore settings may affect local files.

What should I do if I see a save warning?

Check the free space on your device and try saving again. If the warning continues, send its exact text and the steps you took to support.

Clearing app data or reinstalling can erase local progress. Contact support before using those steps to fix a save problem.

Will my progress move to another device?

Do not assume progress will transfer automatically. Contact support before changing devices or removing the existing installation.

Languages and presentation

Which interface languages are available?

The game interface supports Turkish, English and German. You can change the language in the game's settings.

Can I adjust sound and graphics?

The game offers sound and visual-quality settings, reduced motion and a battery-saver option. These let you adjust the presentation to your preferences and device.

Questions about information you send us

If you have a question about a support email, an attachment or another privacy matter, write to the same support address and explain your request. Include only information relevant to that request.
