TipNest Privacy and Support

Muhammed Nedim İpek operates TipNest. Contact nedimiiipek@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Your records

TipNest stores the income, expenses, goals and reminders you enter on your device. If you export a backup or share a record, the destination you select receives that copy. Use Settings to clear records or manage reminders.

Ads and subscriptions

Google AdMob supplies banner ads and may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Use Ad privacy in Settings when available. Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase information and an app-generated identifier to determine whether Plus is active and restore access. TipNest does not receive your payment card number.

Support and privacy requests

Email us with the app version and a description of the issue. We use the information you send to answer the request. You can also ask us about access, correction or deletion of personal information, or contact your data protection authority.
